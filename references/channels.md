# Channel playbooks

## LinkedIn
Default: 80–180 words; preview first 2 lines (~180–220 chars together is a practical heuristic, not platform specification). Hook should reflect the specific story. Mobile line breaks typically every 1–3 sentences. Aim for one genuine question if it follows naturally. No forced numbered frameworks, hashtag farms, generic call to action. If linking a Substack article, give a useful standalone observation and link placement only if requested.

## Substack article
Structure according to argument, not social-media punch lines. Allow longer paragraphs, enough evidence and qualifiers, and room for disagreement. Label firsthand experience versus interpretation versus outside reporting. Check headlines for accuracy and overclaiming. Cite original data when research-based. A note can be more casual and much shorter than a full article.

## Pinned first comment
Add a second observation, complication, or source, not a summary of the post; optionally ask a specific question. Do not manufacture 'insider details.'

## Replies and DMs
Respond to what was actually said. 1–4 sentences is often enough. Acknowledge a meaningful detail, offer a related perspective, ask back only when natural. Avoid 'Great insights!', 'Absolutely!', 'I couldn't agree more!' as empty starters. For connection requests, introduce identity only as much as required; avoid implying endorsement or relationship.

## Serial content
Across 3 posts, vary: openings, actual example, paragraph patterns, question styles, and lengths. Do not repeat the same 'I noticed / I used to think / now I wonder' structure. Maintain one recognizable perspective without mechanical uniformity.
