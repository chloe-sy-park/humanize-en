# Anti-patterns: detect, don't blindly ban

## Strong signs requiring revision unless clearly intentional
- False contrast: 'It's not about X. It's about Y.' where nobody argued X; express Y directly.
- Empty mic-drop closer: 'And that changes everything.' 'Let that sink in.' Replace with a concrete fact or remove.
- Staged opener: 'Here's the thing.' 'I need to talk about...' Lead with observation.
- Vague expert claim: 'Studies show...' 'Everyone knows...' With no sourcing, flag or remove attribution claim, not the underlying uncertainty.
- Recycled authority: 'The key takeaway is...' 'Best practices dictate...' 'Ultimately...' Cut the lecture frame.
- Business buzzword stacks: leverage, unlock, transformative, game-changing, seamless, ecosystem, revolutionize, navigate the landscape. Use actual verbs and details.
- Fake first-person: 'I learned this from my users' without evidence; ask or leave it out.
- Forced CTA: 'Agree?' or 'Thoughts?' when the post has no real dilemma.

## Contextual warnings
- Three perfectly parallel bullets or ideas every time; keep three when truly needed.
- Repeated 'I used to think X. Now I think Y.' across successive posts. Valuable sometimes, stale as a repeated formula.
- Sentences all roughly same length; choppy line breaks every 4–6 words.
- Equal-size intro, example, insight, open question every post. Vary structure when writing a series.
- 'As a founder...' identity signaling when the lived situation already demonstrates it.
- 'I realized...', 'I noticed...', 'I'm curious...' in every paragraph. These are useful in moderation, not stamps of authenticity.
- Overgeneralizing Korea vs global consumers based on one case.
- Excessive disclaimers, repeated 'maybe', or 'I could be wrong' as performative humility.
- Decorative emoji headings, hashtags, excessive bold, title-case subheads.
- Overly clever idioms, aspirational American creator voice, motivational aphorisms.
- Em dashes: replace throughout for this project, even if grammatically acceptable.

## Do not delete
- Small odd details, honest uncertainty, personal asides, genuine disagreements, deliberate repetition, concrete names and amounts, real quotes.
- Normal workplace terms like product, user, onboarding, conversion, SEO, market entry when used precisely.
