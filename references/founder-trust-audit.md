# Founder Trust Audit (v3.1)

This is a practical editing procedure, **not** an AI authorship detector or validated model of reader trust.

## Mandatory checks
1. **Credibility source:** label direct experience, observed behavior, external research, third-party post, interpretation, or speculation. Do not blur categories.
2. **Situation:** if an actual case exists, put the concrete event/constraint before unsupported general claims. Do not demand a personal story when only research is available.
3. **Decision trace:** if the writer's thinking changed, identify what observation justified it. Do not invent a conversion moment.
4. **Scope:** a single customer, post or client case cannot support population-level conclusions. Include geography, sample and timing when meaningful.
5. **Outcomes:** preserve denominators, dates, definitions, measurement limitations and attribution. Reported results ≠ independently verified results; sequence ≠ causation.
6. **Ownership:** consulting on a company, researching a product, operating a brand, and building the underlying product are different roles.
7. **Quote/consent:** no made-up customer dialog. Check whether client identifiers, confidential metrics or screenshots may be shared.
8. **Structure choice:** select from the ten-case atlas only when facts support it. Avoid identical hook → story → lesson → question cadence by default.
9. **Voice:** readable business English for a Korean founder, without artificial grammar mistakes or imitating a named founder.
10. **Reader value:** verify the reader can understand what the author noticed and why; no automatic three lessons, hype, forced advice, or engagement bait.

## Decision labels
- **PASS:** specific firsthand observation or properly scoped sourced analysis; no material distortions.
- **REVISE:** evidence exists but framing exaggerates role, outcome or certainty; rewrite with correct attribution.
- **HOLD:** unsupported central claim, unresolved consent/confidentiality, or a narrative that requires fabricated experience. Request evidence or drop the claim.

## Review procedure
1. Create a 5-column ledger internally: claim | source | author's role | confidence/scope | edit decision.
2. Check first two lines: does the reader know *what happened or what is being examined*? A reflective opener is acceptable if relevant.
3. Mark before/after percentages or results as reported, observational or causal only with appropriate evidence.
4. Run a voice and pattern check using `pattern-audit.md` and `voice-guide.md`.
5. For a series of 3+ posts compare opener type, structure ID, evidence source, paragraph rhythm and ending. Correct clear repetition, not genuine subject continuity.
6. Summarize any unresolved central evidence issues. A strong 50-point score does not override HOLD.

## Notes on benchmark interpretation
- Ten curated examples cannot identify statistically effective structures or predict impressions, conversations or trust.
- A post about a verified event may still include a self-reported interpretation; keep these levels distinct.
- Authorial humility must never soften verified facts or add fake insecurity.
- “US founder voice” here means readability and useful business reasoning for the intended audience, not national, ethnic or linguistic identity.
