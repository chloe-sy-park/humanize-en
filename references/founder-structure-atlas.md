# Founder structure atlas: ten LinkedIn examples (v3.1)

> **Status:** Qualitative editorial reference set assembled from ten public LinkedIn posts discussed with the user. Not a representative sample of American founders, not an outcome study. Names, post content and reported results must not be used as independently verified facts without checking their original sources. Links can change or become inaccessible. Do not quote exact language without reopening the original.

## How to use
1. Choose an evidence category and channel before choosing a structure.
2. Select a structure only when the source contains the necessary ingredients. Missing evidence means no invented anecdote or metrics.
3. Borrow the **logic** of how a post earns understanding, never copy a writer’s distinctive phrasing.
4. When adapting to the author’s experiences, label actual case, research observation, and hypothesis separately.

## Ten case cards

### 01. Difficult founder choice
- **Public example:** Amjad Masad (Replit) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/amjadmasad_in-2021-a-big-tech-company-offered-to-buy-activity-7498417328544485376-Eo3w
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Decision → contemporaneous constraints → trade-off → choice → present reflection
- **Use only when you have:** A verified decision and its actual constraints
- **Evidence limitation:** Personal account of a reported acquisition offer; not independent confirmation of transaction terms
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** Choosing between projects or partnerships under real 2-person-team constraints

### 02. Product future through a user scenario
- **Public example:** Des Traynor (Intercom) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/destraynor_ai-is-making-everything-look-the-same-activity-7386863604333039616-Q9vh
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Product observation → concrete use scenario → limitation → bounded hypothesis
- **Use only when you have:** A real product observation and a plausible, clearly hypothetical example
- **Evidence limitation:** Future-facing argument is a thesis, not a proven prediction
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** AI character chat onboarding or behavioral comparisons with precisely defined scope

### 03. AI mistake and customer trust
- **Public example:** Jason M. Lemkin (SaaStr) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jasonmlemkin_your-ai-agent-just-gave-your-customers-the-activity-7450275510791716864-DQFG
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Reported failure → potential customer impact → response/correction → systemic question
- **Use only when you have:** A documented error, its provenance and outcome
- **Evidence limitation:** Author-reported vendor incident; avoid implying independent incident audit
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** AI character consistency or chatbot mistakes, distinguishing observation from inference

### 04. Customer reality overturns assumption
- **Public example:** Robin Bhaduri (Founder/builder) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/robin-bhaduri-866b2339_startup-ai-founder-activity-7407891550170677248-m0Xl
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Built/tested → customer contact → unexpected feedback → revised assumption
- **Use only when you have:** Actual interview or test notes and consent-safe details
- **Evidence limitation:** Small customer sample; no market generalization
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** Discoverability versus translation in an international customer journey

### 05. Failure and changed founder judgment
- **Public example:** Amanda Zhu (Recall.ai) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/zhu-amanda_my-first-startup-failed-because-i-chose-the-activity-7331696689000484866-rXyN
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Past mistake → responsibility → changed next choice → observed difference
- **Use only when you have:** Real personally owned mistake and an actual later change
- **Evidence limitation:** Self-report; do not manufacture vulnerability to imitate tone
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** A marketing assumption reconsidered across real client engagements

### 06. Product before and after
- **Public example:** Chidi Nweke (Onboarding design) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/chidinweke_a-saas-founder-said-our-retention-is-terrible-activity-7475088060703981568-eQAm
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Friction → intervention → measured/reported outcome → caveat
- **Use only when you have:** Pre/post definitions, timeframe and method; or clear reported-outcome attribution
- **Evidence limitation:** Retention claim is reported by author/client; causal impact unestablished
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** UX and booking-flow improvement, only when measurement exists

### 07. Growth with trade-offs
- **Public example:** Praveen Das (Factors.ai) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/praveen-das_factorsai-startup-founder-activity-7225168506185510912-I8Kp
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Growth metric → hidden cost/constraint → operating decision → qualified hindsight
- **Use only when you have:** True metric units, period, definition, and directly experienced trade-off
- **Evidence limitation:** Post-reported growth is not independently verified
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** Crowdfunding / campaign performance with attribution and limits

### 08. Research-backed pattern observation
- **Public example:** Emmie Chang (Founder audience research) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/emmie_i-spent-last-week-analyzing-the-top-linkedin-activity-7295587533713772544-JrGk
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** What was examined → observed pattern → example or contrast → writer reflection
- **Use only when you have:** Accessible sources, defined sample and actual observed regularities
- **Evidence limitation:** Potential selection bias and nonreplicable sampling
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** Korean AI usage / skincare creator research; label source vs interpretation

### 09. Product launch plus scale constraints
- **Public example:** Des Traynor (Intercom) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/destraynor_our-new-product-operator-goes-live-to-all-activity-7491416542866227200-3137
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Launch fact → actual scale → operational challenge → product decision
- **Use only when you have:** Confirmed launch, audience scope and operational constraint
- **Evidence limitation:** Promotional context; numbers and implications require attribution
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** Pilot workflow vs recurring automation when personally tested

### 10. Customer words vs underlying need
- **Public example:** Ed Kang (Founder/product) — https://www.linkedin.com/posts/edkang99_founders-are-still-getting-talk-to-customers-activity-7452370950349787138-hdDL
- **Observed narrative structure (editorial abstraction):** Common question/assumption → real counterexample → what changed → more precise question
- **Use only when you have:** Actual research or customer insight, with details protected
- **Evidence limitation:** Comment-layer example can be distinct from post; do not present as post-body quote
- **Possible application for this author (not an asserted factual experience):** Cross-border product localization assumptions from actual research

## Structure-selection rule

| Source material available | Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Firsthand decision with documented constraints | 01, 05, 07 | Fake crisis or made-up stakes |
| Firsthand customer/user observation | 03, 04, 06, 10 | Universal market conclusions |
| Market/product research (not owned product) | 02, 08, 10 | “We built/tested” or invented interview |
| Documented release or workflow rollout | 09 | Promotional claims without operational substance |
| Reflection only, limited evidence | Simple work note; conditional 02 | Any before/after numbers or redemption arc |

## Output gate
For any selected structure, the editor should be able to fill: **source → observed fact → author role → inference → limitation**. If a slot is unknown, mark it as unknown or omit it. Reader trust is an editorial objective, not a measurable outcome of these posts.
