# Adversarial review: second editor protocol

## Claim ledger (must run internally for substantial articles and data-driven posts)
For each non-trivial claim log: exact draft claim | type (firsthand / data / sourced report / interpretation / hypothesis) | evidence from provided input | whether language overreaches | fix. No invented external verification.

## The seven challenge questions
1. What does the writer actually know firsthand? What was merely read or inferred?
2. Does a specific anecdote prove only that case, or did the paragraph generalize across Korea, users or AI products?
3. Are numbers consistent in scope, unit, date, comparison base and source?
4. Is the opening honest about the article? Does it promise a lesson/controversy it cannot deliver?
5. Is a 'personal failure' or user conversation included because it happened, or to manufacture vulnerability?
6. Does every sentence sound like something the author could say aloud without rehearsing an investor pitch?
7. If a reasonable skeptic commented, which line would they challenge? Narrow or qualify it, do not hide it.

## No fabricated intimacy
Never invent 'I had a call', 'one customer told me', 'we tested', 'I spent weeks', private emotions or dialogue. A first-person opinion is allowed; the event supporting it must be supplied.

## Repeated content guard
Review 3+ draft posts together when available. Ensure at least two distinct opening mechanisms, variation in ending, and no identical scripted 'before/after realization' structure. Do not introduce synthetic variety for its own sake.

## Stop conditions
If a required firsthand incident or missing verification is central, flag with a precise bracketed author question rather than completing a fictional story. Never silently change statistical facts or citations.
