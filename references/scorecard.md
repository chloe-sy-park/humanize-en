# Editorial scorecard v2 (subjective, 50 points)
Score 1–10 each; do not score drafts not seen.
1. Voice fit: plausible Korean founder speaking about real work, without artificial non-native mistakes.
2. Evidence fidelity: specific claims grounded; uncertainty and ownership accurate.
3. Reader clarity: understandable, direct English, no flashy idioms or needless explanations.
4. Structure/rhythm: human-scale paragraphs and sentences, not formulaic hook/triad/closer.
5. Conversation value: credible reflection, meaningful question if appropriate, not engagement bait.

Interpretation: <35 substantial edits, 35–37 revise, 38–44 usable with polish, 45–50 strong fit. Subjective editorial judgment ONLY, not AI provenance estimation.

## Hard gates (pass/fail independent of score)
- No made-up details or ungrounded metrics and quotation.
- No first-person ownership inflation (consulting ≠ owning client product).
- No altered certainty, causal direction, dates or units.
- Data-driven comparisons have scoped denominator and attribution, or a clear verification flag.
- No accidental em dash in authored prose (verbatim quote exception).
- If revision was requested, do not erase real information while shortening.

## Output audit (only when user explicitly asks for an audit)
Show score before → after with 1-sentence basis; list 3–5 high-impact fixes; note unresolved evidence questions; word counts for constraints. Do not pretend score objectively proves human authorship.
