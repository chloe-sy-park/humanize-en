# Substack: narrower claims, better reasoning

*Fictional writing example. No underlying dataset is implied.*

### Before

Every global brand must localize to succeed. Translation is never enough, and companies that fail to understand local consumers will lose trust.

### After

I used to think an English version of a website would solve most of the first step for international visitors. After looking at a sample navigation flow, I started to question that assumption.

A translated page can still be hard to find. The customer also needs to know where to start and what will happen after clicking a booking button.

This is an example of a usability question, not proof that translation never works. I'd want to compare real user paths before making a broader claim.

### Why this works

The rewrite identifies the narrower claim and the evidence still needed. For publication, replace any first-person description with a verified observation or remove it.
