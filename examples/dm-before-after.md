# DM: avoid generic flattery

*Fictional example; do not send unless the relationship and specific reference are real.*

### Before

Hi! I was deeply inspired by your exceptional visionary work in AI. I'd love to connect and explore synergies.

### After

Hi, I came across your post about onboarding for AI products. I've been thinking about a similar problem from the user side. Would be glad to connect.

### Why this works

It removes unsupported praise and sales language. Only refer to a specific post if you actually read it.
